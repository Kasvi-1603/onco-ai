With the top matched cases as context, Oncopilot helps the oncologist generate all clinical outputs. These are RAG-grounded, not hallucinated.

Treatment plan / chemo orders
AI drafts a regimen citing NCCN guideline + most similar past case. Includes drug name, dose, route, schedule, cycle count, monitoring plan. Oncologist edits inline before signing.
Tumor board (MDT) summary
Compress a 200-page patient history into a 1-page structured brief: demographics, diagnosis timeline, current stage, molecular profile, lines of therapy, and the clinical question for the board (e.g. "Is this patient a candidate for secondary resection?").
Patient-facing summary (plain language)
Translates the pathology + treatment plan into compassionate, jargon-free language. Readability tuned to 8th-grade level. Replaces "poorly differentiated neoplasm with nodal involvement" with "an aggressive cancer that has reached some nearby lymph nodes".
Peer-to-peer / referral letter
Structured letter to the referring GP or specialist. Covers diagnosis, staging, current treatment plan, and next steps. Can be toggled between formal and brief formats.
Clinical trial eligibility report
Checks the patient's biomarker profile against active trials on ClinicalTrials.gov. Lists eligible trials with inclusion/exclusion criteria matched to patient's actual values. Huge value-add oncologists currently do manually.
Toxicity & drug interaction check
Before finalising the treatment plan, flag any contraindications based on the patient's labs, comorbidities, and other medications. Pull from FDA drug labels via RAG.
Prognosis estimate
Based on matched past cases' outcomes (OS, PFS), estimate a probabilistic prognosis range. E.g. "In the 8 most similar past cases, median OS was 14.2 months (range 9–22 months)." Always with uncertainty bands — never a false single number.


The similarity engine is the backbone. It must compare the new patient against all past cases across every measurable parameter simultaneously.

What parameters to compare (each scored separately)
For each parameter, compute a similarity score (0–1). Weighted average gives the final "similarity rank".

Pathology params
Cancer subtype (e.g. Adenocarcinoma)
Tumour grade (G1–G4)
Mitotic index
Surgical margin status
Tumour size (mm)

TP53, STK11, KEAP1

Prior treatment lines

Similarity algorithm (what to build)
For text/categorical data → BioBERT embeddings + cosine similarity. For imaging → pre-trained CNN feature vectors (ResNet-50 fine-tuned on lung CT). For numerical → normalized Euclidean distance. Combine into a weighted multi-modal similarity score. Store all case vectors in FAISS for fast nearest-neighbour search. Top-5 and top-10 matches are shown with a breakdown of which parameters matched and which diverged.
What the oncologist sees in Phase 1
A ranked list of matched past cases. Each card shows: overall similarity %, a parameter-by-parameter match breakdown (green = match, amber = partial, red = diverges), the treatment they received, and outcome (OS, PFS, response). Clickable to drill into the full past case report. Filters by parameter type.
